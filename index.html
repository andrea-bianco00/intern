2. Different Scenarios

This section presents the simulation scenarios considered throughout the study, visualizing the true mean and covariance structures that define each data-generating mechanism.

Different Scenarios

Mean Functions

  • \( \mu_1(t) = 2t^2 \cos(2\pi t) \)
  • \( \mu_2(t) = \sin(8\pi t)\exp(-t) \)
  • \( \mu_3(t) = \sqrt{t(1-t)} \, \sin\left(\frac{2\pi(1+0.05)}{t+0.05}\right) \)

Covariance Scenarios

  • Covariance Scenario I
    Variance function: \( \sigma_X^2(t) = \sqrt{t} \exp\left(-\frac{(t - 0.1)^2}{10}\right) + 1 \)
    Matern correlation function: \( Corr_{\theta=(0.5,1)}(s,t)=\exp(-|s-t|) \)
    Covariance function: \( C(s,t)=\sqrt{\sigma_X^2(s)}\,\sqrt{\sigma_X^2(t)}\,Corr_{\theta=(0.5,1)}(s,t)=\sqrt{\sqrt{s}\exp\left(-\frac{(s-0.1)^2}{10}\right)+1}\,\sqrt{\sqrt{t}\exp\left(-\frac{(t-0.1)^2}{10}\right)+1}\,\exp(-|s-t|) \)
  • Covariance Scenario II
    Fourier basis functions: \( \phi_k(t) = \sqrt{2}\sin\left(2 \pi k t\right) \)
    Variance function: \( \sigma_X^2(t)=\sum_{k=1}^{50}2k^{-2}\phi_k^2(t) \)
    Covariance function: \( C(s,t)=\sum_{k=1}^{50}2k^{-2}\phi_k(s)\phi_k(t) \)
    Correlation function: \( Corr(s,t)=\dfrac{C(s,t)}{\sqrt{\sigma_X^2(s)\sigma_X^2(t)}} \)
  • Covariance Scenario III
    Fourier basis functions: \( \phi_k(t) = \sqrt{2}\sin\left(2 \pi k t\right) \)
    Variance function: \( \sigma_X^2(t)=\sum_{j=1}^{5}\sum_{k=1}^{5}\frac{e^{-|j-k|}}{5}\phi_j(t)\phi_k(t) \)
    Covariance function: \( C(s,t)=\sum_{j=1}^{5}\sum_{k=1}^{5}\frac{e^{-|j-k|}}{5}\phi_j(s)\phi_k(t) \)
    Correlation function: \( Corr(s,t)=\dfrac{C(s,t)}{\sqrt{\sigma_X^2(s)\sigma_X^2(t)}} \)
  • Covariance Scenario IV
    Variance function: \( \sigma_X^2(t) = t \)
    Brownian Motion correlation function: \( Corr_{\theta=(0.5,1)}(s,t)= \frac{(min(s,t))}{\sqrt{s}\sqrt{t}} \)
    Covariance function: \( C(s,t)_{\theta=(0.5,1)}= \min(s,t) \)

4. Total variance function estimation

The comparison reports the estimation of the true total variance function \( \varsigma^2(t) = \sigma_X^2(t) + \sigma_0^2 \) across different covariance scenarios. The total variance estimate is obtained via the mcfda::varfunc procedure, plugging in either the paper mean estimator or the spline mean estimator.
