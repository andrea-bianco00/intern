1. Different Scenarios

This section presents the different scenarios considered in the simulation study. The aim of this section is to provide a clearer understanding of the different simulation settings considered throughout the study. To better interpret the estimation results presented in the following sections, we first visualize the true underlying functional components for each scenario, including the mean and covariance structures. This preliminary overview helps illustrate the complexity and characteristics of the data-generating mechanisms under consideration.
